# Jonathan M. Corcoran · thecorcoran.github.io

A personal collection of working projects, utilities, and ideas.

## Projects & Working Ideas

### I. Digital Reading Rooms & Literature
1. **The Gabriel Marcel Reader** · [Live Reader](https://thecorcoran.github.io/Marcel-Reader/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/Marcel-Reader)
   - Open digital corpus and bilingual reading room for the philosophical and dramatic works of Gabriel Marcel.
2. **Adventure Books** · [Live App](https://thecorcoran.github.io/adventurebooks/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/adventurebooks)
   - Curated digital reading space and audio directory for classical maritime exploration and wilderness journals.
3. **Great Books Audio Directory** · [Live Directory](https://thecorcoran.github.io/GreatBooks/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/GreatBooks)
   - 4-year classical literature and audio directory presented in a clean interface.

### II. Teaching & Classroom Frameworks
4. **The Curriculum of the River** · [Live Site](https://thecorcoran.github.io/MS-Math/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/MS-Math)
   - A 3-year classical middle school mathematics curriculum built on 17" × 11" landscape broadsheets, Socratic sequences, and Thomistic abstraction.
5. **Mini-Book Maker** · [Live App](https://thecorcoran.github.io/MiniBook/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/MiniBook)
   - In-browser print utility formatting 8-page foldable decodable pocket books from a single sheet of paper.

### III. Directories & Cartography
6. **Directory of Catholic Religious Houses** · [Live Directory](https://thecorcoran.github.io/Religious-Houses/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/Religious-Houses)
   - An analytical directory and interactive map cataloging verified autonomous abbeys, monasteries, friaries, and convents across the United States.

### IV. Household & Daily Rhythm
7. **Weekly Schedule Planner (OnTheFrig)** · [Live App](https://thecorcoran.github.io/OnTheFrig/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/OnTheFrig)
   - Minimalist weekly time-blocking and household routine planner formatted for the refrigerator.

### V. Building, Boats & Hardware
8. **Jeff Spira Boat Design Digital Archive** · [Live Archive](https://thecorcoran.github.io/Spira-Boats/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/Spira-Boats)
   - A digital archive collecting and preserving the plywood boat designs, plans, and construction manuals of Jeff Spira.
9. **Building Our Woodshed: Dispatches for Mom** · [YouTube Playlist](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLA4ByYd2SYBedumMs6mpOUhLC3wqhAigT)
   - A 12-part timber construction build journal of an 8' × 40' single-slope woodshed.
10. **AdaWriter (v1.4.3)** · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/AdaWriter)
    - Distraction-free writing instrument powered by a Raspberry Pi and 4.2" e-paper display.
11. **Project Morada** · [Live App](https://thecorcoran.github.io/Morada/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/Morada)
    - Quiet desktop writing environment modeled on Saint Teresa of Ávila’s *Interior Castle*.
