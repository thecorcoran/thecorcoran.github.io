# Jonathan M. Corcoran · thecorcoran.github.io

Personal website, public project hub, and open educational portal for Jonathan M. Corcoran (Olympia, Washington).

## Active Works & Live Sites

1. **The Curriculum of the River** · [Live Site](https://thecorcoran.github.io/MS-Math/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/MS-Math)
   - A 3-year classical middle school mathematics curriculum built on 17" × 11" landscape broadsheets, Socratic sequences, and Thomistic abstraction.

2. **The Gabriel Marcel Reader** · [Live Reader](https://thecorcoran.github.io/Marcel-Reader/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/Marcel-Reader)
   - Open multilingual digital corpus and bilingual reading environment for the philosophical and dramatic works of Gabriel Marcel.

3. **Adventure Books** · [Live App](https://thecorcoran.github.io/adventure-books/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/adventure-books)
   - Curated digital reading sanctuary and audio directory for classical maritime exploration and wilderness journals.

4. **Mini-Book Maker** · [Live App](https://thecorcoran.github.io/mini-book-maker/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/mini-book-maker)
   - In-browser print utility formatting 8-page foldable decodable pocket books from a single sheet of standard paper.

5. **Weekly Schedule Planner (OnTheFrig)** · [Live App](https://thecorcoran.github.io/OnTheFrig/) · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/OnTheFrig)
   - Minimalist weekly time-blocking and homeschool chore planner formatted for the refrigerator.

6. **Project Morada** · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/Project-Morada)
   - Contemplative desktop writing environment modeled on Saint Teresa of Ávila’s *Interior Castle*.

7. **Building Our Woodshed: Dispatches for Mom** · [YouTube Playlist](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLA4ByYd2SYBedumMs6mpOUhLC3wqhAigT)
   - A 12-part timber construction build journal of an 8' × 40' single-slope woodshed on the homestead.

8. **AdaWriter (v1.4.3)** · [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/thecorcoran/AdaWriter)
   - Distraction-free, appliance-style writing instrument powered by a Raspberry Pi and 4.2" e-paper display.
