Biographical Note

About Jonathan M. Corcoran

I am an educator, craftsman, and software builder living with my family on a homestead along the Chehalis Western Trail in Olympia, Washington.

My work unites classical mathematics pedagogy, tactile timber construction, distraction-free appliance computing, and Christian personalist philosophy. I hold degrees in Elementary Education (BA), Mathematics Education (MEd), and Philosophy (MA), and teach middle school mathematics in Washington State.

All projects published here are open-source and freely accessible, designed with durable typography, quiet digital interfaces, and respect for the student, reader, and craftsman.
