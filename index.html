Personal Note

About Jonathan M. Corcoran

I am a husband and father, and I live in the Pacific Northwest. I have lived in Norway and Alaska, but the PNW has always been my home.

I have been a teacher for 20 years. My days and thoughts are shaped by building, seeds, writing, philosophy, theology, the sea, and love. I am also interested in productive property, just law (a bit of an oxymoron), and home economies (also an oxymoron).

This site is a home for working projects, tools, and ideas. Some of these are documented well and ready to explore, while others are in progress. Everything is shared openly.
