Mathematics Pedagogy · Grades 6–8 17" × 11" Broadsheet Folios

The Curriculum of the River

A 3-year classical middle school mathematics curriculum grounded in Thomistic realism, physical craft, and historical metrology.

Replaces fragmented digital exercise sets with rigorous daily landscape broadsheets. Rooted in St. Thomas Aquinas’s epistemic realism (conversio ad phantasmata), every plate moves through four disciplined stages: the Sensory Referent (physical workshop encounter), the Disciplinary Name (authentic academic vocabulary), the Affixing Bridge (operational reversibility), and the Abstract Invariant (universal immutable theorem).

Christian Existentialism · Philosophical Corpus Digital Research Reader

The Gabriel Marcel Reader

An open multilingual reading environment for the philosophy and dramatic works of Gabriel Marcel.

A curated digital reader and research repository dedicated to the French personalist philosopher Gabriel Marcel. Features structured navigation, parallel language alignment, thematic indices, and textual apparatus designed for deep engagement with core Marcelian insights: creative fidelity, intersubjectivity, the mystery of being, and the critical distinction between primary and secondary reflection.

Early Literacy · Print Engine Interactive Tool

Mini-Book Maker

One-sheet 8-page decodable booklet generator and template library.

A web-based imposition and print utility that generates 8-page foldable pocket readers from a single sheet of standard US Letter paper. Features a curated library of 68 phonics-aligned titles across CVC word families, consonant blends, and fine-motor observational drawing prompts, alongside a custom browser editor.

Distraction-Free Hardware Python / Raspberry Pi

AdaWriter (v1.4.3)

Dedicated E-Ink writing instrument with direct physical keyboard interface.

An open-source, appliance-style distraction-free typewriter powered by a Raspberry Pi and a Waveshare 4.2" e-paper display. Uses direct low-level evdev keyboard handling, auto-saving daily journals, monthly log consolidation, local Wi-Fi document transfer, and a peaceful Tolstoy shutdown screen.

About Jonathan M. Corcoran

Jonathan M. Corcoran works at the crossroads of classical mathematics, open-source educational tools, traditional woodworking, and phenomenological philosophy. He holds degrees in Elementary Education (BA), Mathematics Education (MEd), and Philosophy (MA), and teaches middle school mathematics in Washington State.

He resides with his family on a homestead property bordering the Chehalis Western Trail in Olympia, Washington.
